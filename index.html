Sveinn Ólafur Arnórsson

Software engineer in Reykjavík, currently at Arion banki. For over thirty years I've built systems that have to keep working: air traffic simulators, coast guard vessel surveillance, payment gateways, and now the services behind a bank's trading and pension products.

About

I trained as an engineer before I wrote software for a living: mechanical engineering at the University of Iceland, then an MSc in aeronautics and astronautics at Stanford, focused on guidance and control. That background in feedback systems, Kalman filtering and real-time behaviour still shapes how I approach software. I want to know how a system behaves when inputs are noisy, late or wrong.

Since then I've worked in Reykjavík, Copenhagen and Miami, on everything from radar simulation to card-less e-commerce built with the Icelandic banks' data centre. I've also studied financial engineering, which comes in handy now that I build trading and portfolio services.

Away from the keyboard I run and play football.

Exchange integration testing

API-first, service-based design for pension administration in Iceland. Separate services handle members, employers, contributions, funds and payments, with integrations to the tax authority, the financial supervisor and Registers Iceland.

Delivery and platform

AI-assisted engineering
